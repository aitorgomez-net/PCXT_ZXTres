PCXT ZXTres

An IBM PC/XT, Tandy 1000 and IBM PCjr for the Artix-7 ZXTRES boards, built around the MCL86 CPU and the KFPC-XT chipset. It is the machine of the PCXT MiSTer core, with a board layer of its own: the DeMiSTify controller and OSD, the microSD card, VGA and 15 kHz video, and the board's audio.
